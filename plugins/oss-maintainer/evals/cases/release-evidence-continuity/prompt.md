Create a record with `new`, record a source-bound passed check with `set --check`, then atomically add a wheel digest and its artifact-bound check in one `set --digest ... --check ... --on artifact:wheel` call. Show with `show --json` that: a replaced wheel digest resets the artifact check while the source check stays valid; `set --commit <new-sha>` resets the source-bound check and clears the tested digests; and `set --check NAME=passed --reuse-from <id> --reason ...` records a new entry pointing to the original check while preserving the original execution, its evidence and its timestamp. Never record a passed check that did not run; reuse is the only way to carry evidence over. No network, publication or paid calls.
